Meet the concept

A small sensor.
A clearer picture.

CarbonTrack imagines a practical sensor paired with a straightforward digital experience. The goal is to make emissions information easier to access, rather than bury it in technical readings.

  • Designed around everyday vehicle use
  • Clear, easy-to-read information
  • Dashboard concept for tracking trends over time
